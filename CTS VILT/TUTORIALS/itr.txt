If your Income Tax Return (ITR) has been filed and more than 3 months have passed but the refund has not been credited, you can take the following steps:

🔎 Step 1: Check Refund Status
Go to the Income Tax e-Filing Portal.

Login with your PAN and password.

Navigate to "e-File" → "Income Tax Returns" → "View Filed Returns".

Check the status of your return:

Processed with refund → Refund will be issued by CPC (Central Processing Centre).

Processed with demand/adjustment → Refund may have been adjusted against tax dues.

Under processing → Still pending at CPC.

You can also check refund status at TIN NSDL Refund Status portal: https://tin.tin.nsdl.com/oltas/refundstatuslogin.html.

🏦 Step 2: Verify Bank Details
Ensure the bank account linked in your e-filing profile is pre-validated and EVC enabled.

Sometimes refunds fail if account details are incorrect or not pre-validated.

📩 Step 3: Raise a Grievance
If refund is still not credited:

Go to e-Filing Portal → Grievances → Submit Grievance.

Choose category: "Refund not received / Delay in refund".

☎️ Step 4: Contact CPC (Bengaluru)
Helpline: 1800 103 4455 or 080-46605200.

Provide your PAN, Assessment Year, acknowledgment number.

⚖️ Step 5: If Still Not Resolved
File a complaint via CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System): https://pgportal.gov.in/.

As a last resort, approach the Jurisdictional Assessing Officer through the e-filing portal.

✅ In most cases, refunds get delayed due to pending processing at CPC or bank account validation issues.
